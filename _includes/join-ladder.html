{% comment %} The join progression: three ways in, ordered by how much they ask of a family. Deliberately a ladder rather than one call to action. Per the branding guidelines, the registration link belongs in the Join section and "sign up" is correct wording now that the link exists — but the mandated register is "plain, warm, and low-pressure," so a paid national registration is not the lead ask. Visiting costs nothing, the mailing list is the capture that lets us reach a family again, and registration is for people who have already decided. Tier 1 names the next all-pack MEETING, which is the thing a visitor can actually drop in on. Two conditions, both needed: "All-Pack" in the summary (excludes den meetings) and the OLL School location (excludes all-pack outings and field trips — the State Fair and Museum of Flight trips are all-pack but are not somewhere to turn up cold).
